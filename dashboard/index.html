Actionable Lifecycle Insights

Key engineering guidelines extracted from the model and dataset

Environment Severity

CO2 Pipeline Wet and Process Brine are the most aggressive service environments, averaging 5.48 and 3.56 MPY. High-integrity coatings are critical in these systems.

Alloy Durability

Carbon steel suffers the highest baseline corrosion rates (average 3.96 MPY). Upgrading to Duplex 2205 or Ti Grade 2 reduces rates by up to 78%.

Protective Barriers

Applying 3LPE or FBE coatings reduces corrosion rates below 1.32 MPY, preventing rapid wall loss compared to untreated surfaces (5.28 MPY).

Maintenance Impact

Active Cathodic Protection suppresses degradation rates down to 1.39 MPY, significantly outperforming wash intervals or chemical biocide dosing.
